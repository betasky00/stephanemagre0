Private Commission

Cour Urbaine

Location Paris, 7e arrondissement
Scope Full design & execution oversight
Type Private apartment courtyard
Status Completed
Design intent

Small in scale,
precise in every
decision.

This courtyard belongs to a private apartment in the 7th arrondissement. The space is enclosed on all sides, receives limited direct sun, and is visible from the main living areas of the apartment throughout the year. It needed to be beautiful at every season and in every light.

The design accepts the constraints of the site rather than fighting them. The floor was entirely relaid in large-format limestone, set with minimal joints to create a clean, generous plane. A single hornbeam, carefully selected for scale and form, provides the vertical element and the shifting quality of light that the space needed.

Along one wall, a long planting bed contains a considered mix of shade-tolerant species — ferns, hellebores, epimedium, and selected grasses — that provide movement, texture, and seasonal interest without demanding direct sunlight or excessive maintenance.

There are no unnecessary elements. Every detail — the edge conditions, the drainage channel, the single outdoor light fitted into the wall — was considered as part of the whole. The result is a courtyard that feels composed rather than decorated.
