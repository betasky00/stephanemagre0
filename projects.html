Selected commissions

Projects

A careful selection of private landscape commissions. Each project represents a specific dialogue between site, architecture, and the way a place is lived in.

Villa Privée

Île-de-France

A garden designed to create a sequence of calm spaces — geometric structure and restrained planting in dialogue with a contemporary residence.

Résidence

Côte d'Azur

A Mediterranean garden on a sloped site — dry planting, stone terracing, and long views to the sea integrated into a coherent landscape.

Domaine Rural

Normandie

Restoration and redesign of a historic estate — parkland structure, kitchen garden, and woodland edges reconsidered for contemporary use.

Cour Urbaine

Paris, 7e

A private courtyard transformed through material restraint, precise planting, and the careful management of light within a dense urban environment.
