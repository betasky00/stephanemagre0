Private Commission

Villa Privée

Location Île-de-France
Scope Full design & supervision
Type Private residence, 2.4 ha
Status Completed
Design intent

A garden composed
around sequence
and calm.

This project began with a single question: how do you design a garden for a contemporary house that feels as considered as the architecture itself? The clients had recently completed a renovation of their Île-de-France property and wanted the landscape to match the precision and restraint of the interior.

The design is structured around a sequence of spaces that unfold as you move through the garden — an entrance court, a central terrace, a kitchen garden, and a wilder zone at the boundary that transitions towards the surrounding forest. Each space is distinct in character but connected by consistent materials and a calm planting palette of greens, greys, and white.

Stone was sourced locally and cut to custom dimensions. Hedges define structure without enclosing. The circulation was designed to slow movement down and create moments of pause — a quality the clients described as essential to how they want to live on the property.

The project was executed in collaboration with a specialist stone contractor and a selected planting team over two growing seasons, allowing the structure to establish before the more delicate elements were introduced.
