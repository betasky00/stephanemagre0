Private Commission

Résidence

Location Côte d'Azur
Scope Design, terracing & planting
Type Private villa, hillside site
Status Completed
Design intent

Terracing, light,
and the language
of the South.

This property sits on a steep hillside above the coast, with views that demanded respect. The brief was to create outdoor spaces that felt native to the landscape — not imported or imposed — while providing comfortable terraces for daily life and generous entertaining.

The design works with the natural slope rather than against it. A series of stone-retained terraces descend from the house, each with a distinct purpose: a dining terrace close to the kitchen, a pool terrace with long shadow lines in the afternoon, and lower terraces transitioning into wilder planting that meets the hillside vegetation below.

Planting was chosen for resilience and beauty in equal measure. Drought-tolerant species — lavender, rosemary, cistus, garrigue — are combined with controlled accents of olive, stone pine, and agave for structure. The palette was deliberately kept close to the natural colours of the site: grey-green, silver, ochre, and white.

The project was carried out in close collaboration with the architect responsible for the renovation of the house, ensuring a coherent relationship between interior and exterior throughout.
